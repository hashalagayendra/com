# Service section implementation

Placed directly after the hero, before selected work. The supplied image determines the direction; no direction draw or redraw was needed. Its four disciplines become four numbered purple artwork columns. The interactive element is an expandable service detail panel with a working link to the existing contact dialog.

The reference intentionally overrides the design skill's general rules against eyebrows, index numbering, thin dividers, violet, accent words and three-line headings. The source taste linter flags these reference features; they are retained to follow the user's request.

Visual review: desktop 1440px, tablet 768px, mobile 390px. No horizontal overflow; all four images loaded. Desktop uses the reference's five-column composition; tablet places the intro above four cards; mobile wraps the cards into two columns. Screenshots are services-1440.jpg, services-768.jpg and services-390.jpg in this directory. Design 8/10, usability 8/10, creativity 7/10, content 8/10; weighted score 7.8/10. The artwork follows the reference's subjects but is newly generated, rather than an exact reproduction. Additional service details and capabilities are sample copy to review before launch. No external photography or licensing credits are used.
